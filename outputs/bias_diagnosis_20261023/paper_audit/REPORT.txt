Zhao2019 Fig10(b,c): paper/source domain audit (2026-10-23)

Strongest findings
1. Native Fig10 colorbars are LINEAR scattered power. A logarithmic or gamma
   correction of a digitized 0..1 colorbar-position array is unsupported.
2. Paper simulations sum local LARED spectra across a finite scattering volume;
   spectral response/resolution, path absorption and temporal resolution enter.
   Current canonical Fig1/Fig11 training generates single-state clean PV spectra.
   These are substantive forward/observation model differences.
3. The paper fitting convention has one Ti and one ion flow; current synthetic
   sampler permits independent H/C temperatures, flows and distribution shapes.
   This changes the inverse problem and the learned prior. It does not by itself
   prove a particular sign of prediction bias.

Paper physics: explicit statements versus interpretation

Maxwellian/local model:
 PDFp4/journal3, L228-272: collisionless, unmagnetized Maxwellian distributions;
 Eqs(5),(6) are Gaussian electron/ion VDFs; Eqs(7)-(9) their susceptibilities.
 PDFp5/journal4, L321-323: "The spectra are calculated according to Eqs. (1)-(9),
 using the plasma conditions given by the 2D radiation-hydrodynamic code LARED."
 Therefore local simulated spectra use the stated Maxwellian model. A sum of
 local Maxwellian spectra is generally not equal to one homogeneous Maxwellian
 spectrum. No published arbitrary-VDF simulation is described here.

Ion temperature and flow:
 PDFp4, L221-228: "X involves seven variables (Te, Ti, uik, uek, A, B1, B2)".
 Paper spectral fitting shares one Ti and one plasma ion flow. Eq(8) itself has
 generic species Tj; Fig11 reports only one Ti. Equality of all ion temperatures
 in every LARED cell is strongly implied by the reported hydro model but is NOT
 explicitly stated as TH=TC. Do not claim the paper independently measured TH/TC.
 PDFp4,L207-212: ion response uses omega_s-omega_i-k.u; electron response uses
 omega_s-omega_i-k.(u+ud). PDFp9,L524-529: center flow "quite small", small Doppler
 shift, not established as identically zero. No separate H/C flows are reported.

Spatial/optical/time averaging:
 PDFp5,L326-336: scattering volume is overlap of probe beam and diagnostic
 aperture, divided into elements. "Each element is assigned ... (Te, Ti, ne, etc.)"
 and spectra "are calculated separately and then summed."
 PDFp6/journal5,L356-366: "The spectral response, the spectral resolution, and the
 inverse bremsstrahlung absorption ... along the optical path are also involved
 in the calculation." Time-resolved spectra have "temporal resolution accounted
 for." This is finite-volume synthesis with optical-path effects; it is not an
 unbounded line-of-sight integral. Exact weights/mesh/kernels are unpublished.
 PDFp9,L506-510: 90deg scattering, volume~100x100x100um^3; spectral resolution
 0.057nm FWHM. Thus resolution is included in simulation methodology and this
 instrument's resolution is specified. The paper DOES NOT explicitly name a
 Gaussian kernel or disclose a numeric0.057nm convolution implementation.
 Gaussian convolution is a reasonable implementation hypothesis, not a quote.
 PDFp4,L289-293: collection f/10 produces k variance~+/-2.5%, called quite small.
 Fig1 gradient/k-smearing comparison is explicit; exact Fig10 angular quadrature
 is not disclosed.

Density/mixture/charges:
 PDFp9,L537-539: pentane C5H12 at0.35atm corresponds to ne~3.5e20cm^-3 "when
 fully ionized". This is a fill baseline, not local ne fixed for every spectrum.
 PDFp5,L330-334: for Fig1(b)'s4.3ns spherical spectrum, simulated ne spans
 3e20-5e20cm^-3 within the volume. Native Fig1b label ne=4.0e20cm^-3 (Fig1a3.5e20).
 PDFp5 Fig1 caption,L311: fitted density "is assumed according to simulation".
 H:C number ratio12:5 follows stoichiometry. Fully ionized pentane implies
 ZH=1,ZC=6. However the paper does not state time-/cell-resolved charge states,
 exact mixture evolution or gold contamination for Fig10; avoid presenting those
 fully stripped fixed-charge assumptions as verified hydro implementation facts.

Which temperature target:
 PDFp10 Fig10 caption,L565-566: (b)simulation without probe, (c)with probe.
 Fig11 caption,L600-602: red/blue lines from simulations with/without probe;
 squares inferred from experiment; shaded areas measure in-volume inhomogeneity.
 Fig11 center-line temperatures and a fitted effective temperature of a volume-
 averaged spectrum are different targets; equality is not guaranteed.
 PDFp10,L569-584 explicitly discusses simulation/experiment Te discrepancy after
 2.8ns and local Spitzer-Harm conduction. Therefore comparing Fig10 simulated
 spectra directly to experimental square temperatures can itself create bias.

Current synthetic source comparison (read-only source, no generated samples)
data.py213-296,394-456:
 - e/H/C VDFs sampled independently among Maxwellian40%, Gaussian-mixture40%,
   superGaussian20%; independent H/C Ti factors and flow offsets.
 - experimental Fig11 Te and Ti pools independently paired, and Figure1 anchors;
   this does not preserve a hydro simulation trajectory or its correlations.
 - local ne uniformly sampled around experimental density assumptions; density
   affects forward spectrum but is not an appended network input.
 - probe263.3nm,90deg,H:C12:5; physicalconfig inherited charges H1/C6.
 - generate_sample calls normalized PV spectrum directly at target wavelength
   samples. No spectral/temporal response, volume sum or optical-path absorption.
These differences establish potential domain shift. Attribution of any actual
neural temperature bias requires matched input, checkpoint and preprocessing evidence.
No claim that adding response or a commonTi constraint necessarily fixes bias.
